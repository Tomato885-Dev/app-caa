CAA VERBO - NOTES FOR APP REVIEW

WHAT THE APP IS
CAA Verbo is the official app of the Student Council (Centro de Alumnos) of
Colegio Del Verbo Divino, a secondary school in Santiago, Chile. Its audience
is the school community: about 700 students aged 13 to 18, plus teachers and
staff. The app is free: no advertising, no purchases, no
subscriptions, no tracking.

ACCESS IS CLOSED
An account can only be created by someone whose institutional e-mail address
(@verbo.cl) is on the school's official roster. There is no open sign-up. The
demo account provided in the sign-in fields above is a regular student
account, hidden from the contact directory. A code by e-mail is required only when
CREATING an account, never when signing in, so the demo account needs no
mailbox access.

WHERE THINGS ARE
Inicio (home), Comunicados (announcements), Noticias (news), Eventos (events
and sign-ups), Calendario, Casino (the canteen's weekly menu), 365 (inter-grade
competition results), Proyectos, Central de apuntes (each year group's shared
Drive folder of study notes), Colaboradores (the businesses that support the
Council), Contactos (directory), Mi perfil.
- Push notifications: Mi perfil, section "Avisos". Sent only when the Student
  Council publishes something; never marketing.
- Account deletion: Mi perfil, "Eliminar mi cuenta", done inside the app.
- Privacy policy: in Mi perfil, and again beside the phone number field.

NO USER-GENERATED CONTENT
Students cannot publish anything. Only the Council's elected board and the
teams appointed by the school can, and only into the app's own sections. There
are no comments, no private messaging, no photo uploads and no public
profiles. The only text a student can write anywhere in the app is
their own optional phone number; their name and class come from the roster and
cannot be edited, and anyone can hide themselves from the directory entirely.
There is therefore no user-to-user content to report or block.

NO PAID CONTENT
All features are free: no in-app purchase, subscription or paywall.
Colaboradores lists the businesses that support the Council; the
app takes no payment and issues no voucher or code.

EXTERNAL SERVICES: ONLY TWO
1. Supabase - database, authentication, file storage, serverless functions and
   the confirmation e-mails sent at registration. Hosted on AWS.
2. Firebase Cloud Messaging with APNs - push delivery only.
No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising network or identifier, no
payment processor and no AI service. No personal data is sold, shared or
transferred to anyone.

REGIONS
No regional differences, no geolocation, no region gating. Spanish only.

THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry: no financial, medical, gambling or dating services. Text, photographs and logos belong to the school and its Council;
partner logos are shown with each business's agreement. News and events may
carry a video the Council links from YouTube, Vimeo or Google Drive: on device
it opens in an in-app browser at its source.

GUIDELINE 3.2
This is not a business or employee app. Its users are students who install it
on their own personal iPhones, which the school neither owns nor manages. With
no MDM and no managed Apple Accounts, custom distribution through Apple
Business Manager would leave the intended users unable to install it. It is a
school community app, free and with no commercial purpose.

WHAT IS NEW IN 1.13
Version 1.12 was built but never submitted, so this release carries both.
Fixed: after switching away from the app and returning, it could stop
responding until relaunched. Colaboradores dropped its voucher system and now
simply presents each partner. The canteen block on the home screen switches to
tomorrow's menu at 2 pm. Faster contact directory, a clear offline notice and a
redesigned study notes section. A screen reserved for administrators can move
every class up one school year; it is not reachable from the demo account,
which is a student account.
