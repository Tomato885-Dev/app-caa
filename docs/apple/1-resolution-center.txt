2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
App CAA is the official app of the Student Council (Centro de Alumnos) of
Colegio Del Verbo Divino, a secondary school in Santiago, Chile. Its audience
is the school's own community: around 700 students aged 13 to 18, plus
teachers and staff. It is not aimed at the general public.
Until now the Council's announcements travelled through hallway posters,
WhatsApp groups and word of mouth, arriving late or not at all. The app gives
the school one official channel: announcements, news, events and sign-ups, the
calendar, inter-grade competition results, Council projects, partner discounts
and a contact directory. It is free: no advertising, purchases or tracking.

3. ACCESS AND MAIN FEATURES
Access is closed: an account can only be created by someone whose
institutional e-mail (@verbo.cl) is on the school's official roster. There is
no open sign-up. A code by e-mail is required only when CREATING an account,
never when signing in, so the demo account needs no mailbox.

Sections: Inicio, Comunicados, Noticias, Eventos, Calendario, 365, Proyectos,
Colaboradores (partner discounts, each with its code), Contactos, Mi perfil.
Push notifications: Mi perfil, "Avisos"; sent only when the Council publishes,
never marketing. Account deletion: Mi perfil, "Eliminar mi cuenta", inside the
app.
NO USER-GENERATED CONTENT. Students cannot publish. Only the Council's board
and teams appointed by the school can, and only into the app's own sections.
No comments, no messaging, no photo uploads, no public profiles. The only text
a student can write anywhere is their own optional phone number; name and
class come from the roster and cannot be edited, and anyone can hide
themselves from the directory. There is no user-to-user content to report or
block.
NO PAID CONTENT. Every feature is free for every account.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Two only. Supabase (supabase.com): database, authentication, file storage,
serverless functions, and the confirmation e-mails sent at registration;
hosted on AWS. Firebase Cloud Messaging with APNs: push delivery only.
No analytics, crash reporting, advertising network or identifier, payment
processor, AI service or third-party data provider. No personal data is sold,
shared or transferred to anyone.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Same features and content everywhere, no geolocation, no region gating.
Spanish only.

App CAA is not a business or employee app: its users are students installing
